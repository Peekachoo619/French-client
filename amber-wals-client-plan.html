What you will judge (from your brief)
  • The hook. First 3 seconds or it's dead.
  • The door vs. the room. Sell settling foundation and the morning mirror, never "one more anti-wrinkle cream".
  • Product integration. Bottle at true scale, scene-matched light, stable across shots, label readable, fingers crossing it without smearing.
  • Editing rhythm. Cuts, breathing room, sound design. Must hold 30 s.
Hard rulesNo competitor names or third-party logos on screen. Morocco is always "southern Morocco", never "Mediterranean".
Spec9:16, 1080×1920 minimum, 25–60 s, US English, captions burned in and readable muted, MP4.
ClaimsWe stay on the label: smooths, firms, illuminates. No medical wording, so the ads stay safe on Meta and TikTok in the US.
The benchmark · "this is the level" · 40.3 s

23 cuts in 40 seconds

Average shot is 1.7 s. It never stays on one source long: UGC face, origin field, texture macro, 3D skin diagram, before/after, a second and third creator, then offer and unboxing. Captions are 2–4 words, white on a black box, centered. Note it's delivered at 720×1280; our spec asks for more.

What we take, and what we don'tWe take the rhythm (1.5–2.5 s shots), the origin-to-texture-to-face loop, the multi-source feel and the caption style. We don't take its hook, its "stop buying lab sludge" enemy line or its structure beat for beat.

A morning at the mirror, not a dermatologist's office.

Keyframes · the still image that starts each clip

Nine images, generated in this order

Each image becomes the first frame of a video clip. The two presenters are generated first and locked, so their faces stay the same in every shot. Full prompts stay in my production files.

Label check on every imageZoom to 100% and read the label: "Amber Wals", "PRICKLY PEAR SEED OIL", "100% Cold-Pressed Virgin Oil", "Sourced in Morocco". If any letter is wrong, the image is regenerated or the real label is placed in post.
Seedance 2.5 · image-to-video · 9:16 · 1080p

Ten clips, each cut into 2–4 shots in the edit

Each clip is written as a locked spec: what each reference controls, the camera, second-by-second action, audio, and what must never happen (warped hands, a changing label, a bottle that shifts size). Full prompts stay in my production files.

Scale lockA 30 mL Boston round with dropper stands about 9.5 cm tall, roughly the length of an adult index finger. Any take where the bottle is out of scale gets rejected.
